# Offers and prices (frozen 2026-10-10, before any recruitment)

These are validation hypotheses, not three products. Cold stays as is; Offers B and C use manual labour. Prices below are **hypotheses**, chosen from scope and delivery cost, not market-proven. If a price is changed after first use, record it as a new row in "Price test log" and report that test separately. Do not overwrite.

Effort and cost figures are **estimates by the author, not measured**. Measure actual hours during delivery and record them in the tracker.

Operator to confirm before use: prices, currency (USD assumed), payment method (no new paid service is approved; use whatever the operator already has, e.g. an existing invoicing account), and refund terms.

## Offer A: Outreach tune-up
- **Problem:** founder already knows whom to contact; writing relevant messages is slow or weak.
- **Price:** USD 150 flat.
- **Deliverables:** (1) 45-minute working session. (2) Founder's product knowledge entered into Cold. (3) Drafts for up to 10 founder-supplied prospects. (4) Side-by-side with the founder's current method or a saved general-AI prompt on 3 of those prospects. (5) One revision round. (6) The knowledge base exported as plain text for the founder to keep.
- **Limits / exclusions:** founder supplies prospects and the "why them" for each; no research; no sending; no reply guarantee; no more than 10 drafts; one revision round.
- **Timeframe:** delivered within 5 calendar days of payment.
- **Founder responsibilities:** supply 10 prospects with a real reason for each; attend the session; review drafts within 48 h; send messages themselves.
- **Refund:** full refund if they decline all drafts as unusable after the revision round (a stated reason is recorded).
- **Estimated effort / direct cost:** about 2.5 h operator time; model cost about 20 calls (unmeasured, expected to be a few dollars at most).
- **Rationale:** priced just above Lavender Starter ($27–29/mo, secondary source) and a month of a general assistant ($20), but as a one-off with human help; low enough to make a "yes" cheap, so it tests whether the writing workflow alone gets any money.
- **Retain / change evidence:** retain if ≥ 2 founders pay at this price. Treat "yes at $150 but no at $300" as only that.

## Offer B: Prospect shortlist
- **Problem:** founder cannot identify relevant potential customers or articulate why they fit.
- **Price:** USD 300 flat.
- **Deliverables:** shortlist of 15 named prospects (people or accounts) from public sources only. Each row has: role/company, the public source (URL and date) showing relevance, the stated reason they would care, and a confidence rating. One 30-minute review call. One replacement round for up to 5 rejected prospects.
- **Exclusions:** no personal emails, phone numbers or scraped contact data; no private data; no enrichment tools; no outreach; no guarantee of fit, replies or sales.
- **Timeframe:** 7 calendar days from payment and receipt of the ICP brief.
- **Founder responsibilities:** 30-minute ICP brief; review within 3 days; label each prospect relevant / not relevant and why.
- **Refund:** full refund if fewer than 8 of 15 are marked relevant after the replacement round.
- **Estimated effort / direct cost:** about 8 h operator time (about 25 min per prospect); no direct cost beyond the operator's existing tools.
- **Rationale:** about USD 37/h of operator time; far below the appointment-setting agencies ($3k–8k/mo, secondary source) and above tool tiers. The acceptance threshold (≥ 8 of 15) is the quality test.
- **Retain / change evidence:** retain if ≥ 2 pay; if founders say they'd pay only for volume, record the stated number.

## Offer C: Customer-conversation sprint
- **Problem:** founder needs to turn product knowledge and a relevant list into real customer conversations.
- **Price:** USD 500 flat (the PR-1 hypothesis).
- **Deliverables:** (1) 45-min kickoff to agree ICP and one outreach objective (e.g. customer-discovery conversation). (2) Shortlist of 20 prospects (up to 10 supplied by the founder, rest researched from public sources). (3) Cold drafts for all 20, one revision round. (4) Two 30-minute review sessions (day 7 and day 14). (5) Reply and next-step tracking in a shared sheet. (6) Written one-page results and next-action recommendation at day 14.
- **Exclusions:** the founder sends every message from their own account; no automation, sequencing or LinkedIn bots; no more than 20 prospects and one follow-up per prospect; no guaranteed replies, meetings or revenue.
- **Timeframe:** 14 calendar days from kickoff.
- **Founder responsibilities:** send within 3 days of approval; log replies within 48 h; join both reviews.
- **Refund:** full refund before shortlist delivery; after that none, because labour is spent. Outcome is not guaranteed. State this in writing.
- **Estimated effort / direct cost:** about 12 h operator time; model cost small (unmeasured).
- **Rationale:** about USD 42/h; includes more labour than A or B but the founder sends, so the cost is bounded. Tests whether a defined outcome sells better than the pieces.
- **Retain / change evidence:** retain if ≥ 2 pay; the pilot's measured reply and conversation counts are reported as outcomes, not as proof of a rate.

## Order of presentation
Show only the offer that matches the demonstrated problem: A if the founder knows whom to contact; B if they don't; C if they have both gaps or ask for a full process. Record which offer was shown and why. Never present all three as a menu to steer a sale.
