# Pre-registered decision criteria (frozen 2026-10-10)

Counts always include denominators. The sample is a small exploratory convenience sample and is **not** representative of founders.

## Definitions
- **Qualified founder:** bootstrapped B2B, live or beta product, fewer than 20 paying customers, has tried to acquire customers in the last 60 days, and describes a specific recent attempt (question 1 of the interview).
- **Paid:** money received. A commitment to pay is not payment. Report separately.
- **Commitment:** specific offer, price, scope and payment date agreed in writing.
- **Concrete next step:** a dated action by the founder (e.g. send the ICP brief by a date) after seeing the offer.
- Evidence labels in the tracker: `observed` (I saw it), `self-reported`, `inferred`.
- Independent means different founders, not sourced from the same thread or introduction.

## Target funnel (targets, not assumed results)
25 personalized messages, then 8-10 qualified interviews, then offers shown to qualified founders who show a relevant problem.

## Pass: proceed to a narrowly scoped paid pilot
All of:
1. At least 2 paid pilots, or a documented equivalent (at least 4 written commitments with date and price).
2. At least 3 independent qualified founders describe the same urgent problem with a recent concrete attempt.
3. Evidence supports one segment and one repeatable offer (≥ 2 purchases/commitments for the same offer).
4. Any differentiation claim requires that, in Offer A comparisons, a majority of founders (and at least 4 comparisons) pick Cold over their alternative as the draft they would actually send. Preference alone does not show reply-rate improvement.
5. No trust or security issue blocks safe delivery.

## Uncertain: narrow and retest
Any of: strong problem evidence but < 2 payments; most qualified founders name an upstream problem (prospect selection or positioning); offer, price or scope mismatch evidenced by stated objections.

## Fail: stop or reconsider
Any of: after ≥ 8 qualified interviews with offers shown, zero payments and fewer than 2 commitments; a majority of qualified founders consider existing tools sufficient; the problem is infrequent, low-value or unreachable; no viable segment or offer.

## Inconclusive
If fewer than 8 qualified interviews are completed, or offers were shown to fewer than 5 qualified founders, the result is **inconclusive** unless the available evidence independently supports another conclusion (state which).

## Time box
Recruitment and interviews: 14 days from first message. Offers: within 7 days of each interview. Pilots: the offer's own timeframe. The report is updated at the end of each stage.
